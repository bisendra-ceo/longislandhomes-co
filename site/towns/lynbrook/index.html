Buying a home in Lynbrook, NY

By Bisendra Melaram, SRES, Licensed Real Estate Salesperson, Keller Williams Greater Nassau. Last updated November 8, 2026.

Lynbrook is an incorporated village of about two square miles in the Town of Hempstead, with 20,438 residents in 2020. Lynbrook station, on Sunrise Highway, is served by Long Beach Branch trains and some Babylon Branch trains. Five school districts serve parts of the village, so the district depends on the exact address.

How do you commute from Lynbrook to Manhattan?

Lynbrook station sits off Sunrise Highway between Peninsula Boulevard and Broadway. It is served by Long Beach Branch trains and select Babylon Branch trains. Under the MTA's 2023 service plan, Long Beach Branch peak trains run to both Penn Station and Grand Central Madison. Westwood station on the West Hempstead Branch straddles the Lynbrook and Malverne border.

Is Lynbrook in a flood zone?

The Census counts Lynbrook's area as all land, with no water inside the village. That does not rule out flood zones on a given lot. Look up the exact address on FEMA's map before you bid.

Since March 20, 2024, New York sellers must answer flood questions on the property condition disclosure, including whether the home is in a FEMA flood zone, whether flood insurance is required, and whether there have been flood claims. Read our South Shore flood zone guide for how to check a house.

Is Lynbrook a village or part of the Town of Hempstead?

Lynbrook is an incorporated village with its own government and village taxes, on top of Town of Hempstead, Nassau County and school taxes.

Which school district serves a home in Lynbrook?

Five public school districts serve parts of Lynbrook: Lynbrook, East Rockaway, Hewlett-Woodmere, Malverne, and the Valley Stream districts, including Valley Stream Central High School District. The district depends on where in the village the home sits, so confirm it for the exact address.

Questions buyers ask about Lynbrook

Is Lynbrook a village?

Yes. Lynbrook incorporated as a village in 1911 and sits in the Town of Hempstead, Nassau County.

Which LIRR branch serves Lynbrook?

Lynbrook station is served by Long Beach Branch trains and select Babylon Branch trains. Westwood station, on the West Hempstead Branch, sits at the Lynbrook and Malverne border.

What school district is Lynbrook in?

Five districts serve parts of Lynbrook: Lynbrook, East Rockaway, Hewlett-Woodmere, Malverne and the Valley Stream districts. The district depends on the address.
