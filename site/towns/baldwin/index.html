Buying a home in Baldwin, NY

By Bisendra Melaram, SRES, Licensed Real Estate Salesperson, Keller Williams Greater Nassau. Last updated November 8, 2026.

Baldwin is an unincorporated hamlet in the Town of Hempstead, with 33,919 residents in 2020. Its LIRR station is on the Babylon Branch. Most of Baldwin is in the Baldwin school district, with small parts in the Oceanside, Rockville Centre, Roosevelt and Uniondale districts. The hamlet sits between Milburn Creek and Parsonage Creek.

Is Baldwin a village or part of the Town of Hempstead?

Baldwin is a hamlet, not a village, so there is no village government or village tax. Zoning, permits and building questions go to the Town of Hempstead.

Which school district serves a home in Baldwin?

Most of Baldwin is in the Baldwin Union Free School District. Small parts fall in the Oceanside, Rockville Centre, Roosevelt and Uniondale districts. Confirm the district for the exact address.
