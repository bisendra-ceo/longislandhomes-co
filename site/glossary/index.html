South Shore real estate glossary

By Bisendra Melaram, SRS, PSA, SRES, ABR, SFR, MRP, AHWD, RSPS, Licensed Real Estate Salesperson, Keller Williams Greater Nassau. Last updated November 8, 2026.

This glossary defines the terms sellers and buyers meet on Long Island's South Shore: villages and hamlets, FEMA flood zones and elevation certificates, New York's property condition disclosure, Nassau County assessments and grievances, STAR, transfer and mansion taxes, LIRR terms, and the market measures OneKey MLS reports each month.

Incorporated village
A municipality inside a New York town with its own elected government, building department and village tax. Valley Stream, Lynbrook, Rockville Centre and Freeport are incorporated villages in the Town of Hempstead.
Hamlet
An unincorporated community with no village government. Zoning, permits and town services come from the town. Oceanside and Baldwin are hamlets in the Town of Hempstead.
Town of Hempstead
The Nassau County town that contains all six South Shore communities in these guides. Its Receiver of Taxes collects school and general property taxes. Source.
Special flood hazard area
FEMA's high-risk flood zone, often called the 100-year floodplain, with a 1 percent chance of flooding in any year. Mortgages on homes in it can require flood insurance. Source.
Moderate flood hazard area
FEMA's moderate-risk zone, often called the 500-year floodplain, with a 0.2 percent chance of flooding in any year. Source.
Elevation certificate
A document from a licensed surveyor or engineer that records a building's elevation against expected flood levels. Insurers can use it to rate flood insurance, and New York's disclosure form asks whether one exists. Source.
Property condition disclosure statement
The form New York sellers of most one to four family homes must give buyers before the buyer signs a contract. Since March 20, 2024 it cannot be replaced by a $500 credit, and it includes flood questions. Source.
Assessment Review Commission (ARC)
The Nassau County agency that hears property assessment appeals, often called grievances. The next filing window runs January 4 to March 1, 2027. Source.
AROW
Assessment Review On the Web, ARC's online system for filing a Nassau County assessment appeal. Source.
Level of assessment
The share of market value a county uses for assessed value. Nassau County's is 0.1 percent, so assessed value is the county's market value estimate divided by 1,000. Source.
STAR credit
New York's School Tax Relief benefit for a primary residence. New owners register for the STAR credit with the state tax department once the home is their primary residence. Source.
Real estate transfer tax
New York's tax on a sale of real property: $2 for each $500 of the price, paid by the seller by default. Source.
Mansion tax
An extra 1 percent New York tax on residences sold for $1 million or more, paid by the buyer. Source.
Union free school district
A form of New York public school district, often shortened to UFSD. On the South Shore district lines do not follow village or hamlet lines.
Grand Central Madison
The LIRR terminal under Grand Central Terminal in Manhattan, the second Manhattan destination besides Penn Station. Source.
Direct train
In these guides, a train that reaches Penn Station or Grand Central Madison without a change at Jamaica. Source.
Closed sale
A sale that has gone to closing, with the deed transferred. Closed sales show what buyers actually paid, unlike asking prices. Source.
Days on market
In OneKey MLS reports, days on market until sale: how long the homes that sold were on the market. Source.
Percent of original list price received
The sale price divided by the first list price, as OneKey MLS reports it. Above 100 percent means homes sold above their original asking price. Source.
Buyer agreement
A written agreement between a buyer and their agent. Since August 17, 2024, agents who are REALTORS need one before touring a home with a buyer. Source.
Surrogate's Court
The New York court that handles estates, including probate and administration, which give an executor or administrator authority to sell a home. Source.
90-day pre-foreclosure notice
The notice New York lenders must send a homeowner at least 90 days before starting a foreclosure on a home loan. Source.

This guide describes places, not people. Fair housing law bars agents from steering buyers toward or away from any neighborhood, and the choice of where to live is yours.
