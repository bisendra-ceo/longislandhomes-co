Valley Stream, NY real estate guide

By Bisendra Melaram, SRS, PSA, SRES, ABR, SFR, MRP, AHWD, RSPS, Licensed Real Estate Salesperson, Keller Williams Greater Nassau. Last updated November 8, 2026.

Valley Stream is an incorporated village in the Town of Hempstead on the Queens border, with 40,634 residents in 2020. It has two LIRR stations, Valley Stream and Gibson, on the Far Rockaway and Long Beach branches. Elementary schools come from three districts, 13, 24 and 30, and high school from Valley Stream Central.

What should sellers in Valley Stream know?

Buyers looking at Valley Stream ask about the same few things this page covers: the flood zone, the school district, taxes and the commute. Have your answers ready before you list.

  • Price from closed sales in your part of Valley Stream, not asking prices. OneKey MLS reports by county, and Nassau County's monthly median single-family price ran from $835,000 to $911,000 in 2026 through September.
  • Complete the New York property condition disclosure, flood questions included, before your first showing. Since March 20, 2024 it cannot be replaced with a $500 credit.
  • Name the right district. Three elementary districts and part of Hewlett-Woodmere serve the village, and the disclosure form asks you which one serves your home.
  • Have your tax bills ready. Prepaid or unpaid taxes are adjusted with the buyer at closing, and buyers budget by monthly cost.

What should buyers in Valley Stream know?

  • Look up the exact address on FEMA's Flood Map Service Center. FEMA says nearly 25 percent of flood insurance claims come from outside high-risk flood zones, so a low-risk map is not the end of the question.
  • Confirm the school district for the street with the district, not the listing.
  • Two stations serve the village. Valley Stream station has the faster direct morning trains, and Gibson has fewer.
  • Since August 17, 2024, agents who are REALTORS need a written buyer agreement before touring a home with you.
  • Register for New York's STAR credit with the state tax department once the home is your primary residence.

How do you commute from Valley Stream to Manhattan?

Valley Stream station is served by Far Rockaway Branch and Long Beach Branch trains. West Hempstead Branch trains stopped calling there in February 2023. Gibson station, in the Gibson section of the village, is on the Far Rockaway Branch. Under the MTA's 2023 service plan, Long Beach Branch peak trains run to both Penn Station and Grand Central Madison.

Is Valley Stream in a flood zone?

Valley Stream sits back from the bays compared with Oceanside or Freeport, but FEMA maps flood risk lot by lot, not town by town. Check the exact address before you bid.

Since March 20, 2024, New York sellers must answer flood questions on the property condition disclosure, including whether the home is in a FEMA flood zone, whether flood insurance is required, and whether there have been flood claims. Is Valley Stream a village or part of the Town of Hempstead?

Valley Stream is an incorporated village, so it has its own mayor, board, building department and village taxes, on top of Town of Hempstead, Nassau County and school taxes.

Which school district serves a home in Valley Stream?

Valley Stream is unusual: elementary students attend one of three separate districts, 13, 24 or 30, depending on the street, and then go on to the Valley Stream Central High School District. Some homes in the southern part of the village are zoned to Hewlett-Woodmere instead. Confirm the district for the exact address with the district office.

Districts that serve parts of Valley Stream, with official sites where checked:

  • Valley Stream Union Free School District 24
  • Valley Stream Union Free School District 30
  • Valley Stream Central High School District
  • Hewlett-Woodmere Union Free School District

How do property taxes work in Valley Stream?

Valley Stream is an incorporated village, so a home there pays village tax on top of Town of Hempstead, Nassau County and school taxes. The village bills its own tax separately.

The Town of Hempstead Receiver of Taxes collects school and general taxes in halves. General taxes are due January 1 and July 1, payable without penalty through February 10 and August 10. School taxes are due October 1 and April 1, payable without penalty through November 10 and May 10.

Nassau County assesses homes at 0.1 percent of its market value estimate. Owners who think the estimate is too high can grieve with the county's Assessment Review Commission. The next window runs January 4 to March 1, 2027, online through AROW. Sellers can read how property taxes and grievances affect a sale.

What kinds of homes are in Valley Stream?

The Census Bureau counts 12,957 housing units in Valley Stream. Owners there estimated the median value of their homes at $616,100, in the American Community Survey 2020 to 2024 five-year estimates. That figure is what owners said, averaged over five years, so it lags the market and is not a sale price. For sale prices, use closed sales.

What do people ask about Valley Stream?

Is Valley Stream a village?

Yes. Valley Stream incorporated as a village on February 14, 1925. It sits inside the Town of Hempstead in Nassau County, along the Queens border.

Which LIRR stations serve Valley Stream?

Valley Stream station, served by the Far Rockaway and Long Beach branches, and Gibson station on the Far Rockaway Branch. Valley Stream station is in fare zone 4.

Which school district is a Valley Stream home in?

It depends on the street. Elementary grades come from Valley Stream 13, 24 or 30, high school from Valley Stream Central, and some southern streets are in Hewlett-Woodmere.

Is Valley Stream in a flood zone?

Some properties may be and many are not. FEMA maps flood zones by address, so look up the exact property on FEMA's Flood Map Service Center and read the seller's flood disclosure.
