What is the Nassau County market doing right now?

In September 2026 the median Nassau County single-family home sold for $860,000, up 2.1 percent from a year earlier, after a median 40 days on market. Sellers received 99.8 percent of their original asking price, according to the OneKey MLS Nassau County report. OneKey publishes these figures by county, so town pages show county numbers until town figures are added.

Why do flood zones matter so much here?

Many South Shore homes sit near bays, creeks and canals, and a FEMA flood zone can mean required flood insurance. New York sellers have had to answer flood questions on the property condition disclosure since March 20, 2024. Start with the South Shore flood zone guide.

Why does the school district depend on the street?

School district lines on the South Shore do not follow town names. Part of Rockville Centre is in the Oceanside district, parts of Lynbrook are in five different districts, and Valley Stream has three elementary districts. Each town guide lists the districts that serve it.

Village or hamlet: what is the difference?

Valley Stream, Lynbrook, Rockville Centre and Freeport are incorporated villages, with their own governments and village taxes. Oceanside and Baldwin are hamlets governed by the Town of Hempstead. Rockville Centre and Freeport also run their own electric utilities.

What else do people ask?

What is the South Shore of Long Island?

The South Shore is the stretch of Long Island along the Atlantic side, facing the bays behind the barrier islands. In Nassau County it runs roughly from Valley Stream on the Queens border east through Massapequa, and it continues through Suffolk County.

What should I check before buying a house on the South Shore?

Check the FEMA flood zone for the exact address, read the seller's flood disclosure, get a flood insurance quote, confirm the school district for the street, and find out whether the home is in an incorporated village with its own taxes.

What should I do before selling a house on the South Shore?

Price from recent closed sales near you, not asking prices. Complete the New York property condition disclosure, including its flood questions, and gather your tax bills, any flood insurance policy, an elevation certificate if you have one, and permits for past work.
