Buying a home in Rockville Centre, NY

By Bisendra Melaram, SRES, Licensed Real Estate Salesperson, Keller Williams Greater Nassau. Last updated November 8, 2026.

Rockville Centre is an incorporated village in the Town of Hempstead, with 26,016 residents in 2020. Its LIRR station is on the Babylon Branch. Most homes are in the Rockville Centre school district, but about a fifth of residents are in the Oceanside district and some are in Baldwin's. The village runs its own electric utility.

Is Rockville Centre in a flood zone?

Most of Rockville Centre sits north of the bayfront towns, but parts of the village do contain water area, and FEMA maps flood zones lot by lot. Check the exact address before you bid.

Since March 20, 2024, New York sellers must answer flood questions on the property condition disclosure, including whether the home is in a FEMA flood zone, whether flood insurance is required, and whether there have been flood claims. Read our South Shore flood zone guide for how to check a house.

Is Rockville Centre a village or part of the Town of Hempstead?

Rockville Centre is an incorporated village with its own government and village taxes. It also runs its own electric department, which the village says lets it offer low-cost power to customers. Ask the seller for recent electric bills.

Which school district serves a home in Rockville Centre?

Most of the village is in the Rockville Centre Union Free School District, which also reaches into parts of South Hempstead. About 20 percent of residents live in the Oceanside district and part of the village is in the Baldwin district. Two houses on nearby streets can pay different school taxes, so confirm the district for the exact address.

Questions buyers ask about Rockville Centre

Is all of Rockville Centre in the Rockville Centre school district?

No. About 20 percent of residents are in the Oceanside school district and part of the village is in the Baldwin district. Confirm the district for the exact address.

Which LIRR branch serves Rockville Centre?

The Babylon Branch. Rockville Centre station is also signed for Molloy.

Who supplies electricity in Rockville Centre?

The village's own Rockville Centre Electric Department, one of three municipal electric utilities on Long Island.

When did Rockville Centre become a village?

Rockville Centre incorporated on July 15, 1893.
