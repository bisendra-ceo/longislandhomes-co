About this guide

Long Island Homes is a free buyer guide to the South Shore of Long Island, written by Bisendra Melaram, a New York Licensed Real Estate Salesperson since 2003 and Seniors Real Estate Specialist with Keller Williams Greater Nassau in Garden City. Each town page cites its sources and carries the date it was last updated.

How are the facts checked?

Town facts come from public sources listed at the bottom of each page: Census figures, LIRR and MTA information, FEMA flood maps, village and school district sites, and New York law. Market numbers come from Bisendra's own analysis of OneKey MLS closed sales. Pages are refreshed monthly.

How does this guide handle fair housing?

It describes places, never the people who live in them. Fair housing law bars agents from steering anyone toward or away from a neighborhood.
