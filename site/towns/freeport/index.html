Freeport, NY real estate guide

By Bisendra Melaram, SRS, PSA, SRES, ABR, SFR, MRP, AHWD, RSPS, Licensed Real Estate Salesperson, Keller Williams Greater Nassau. Last updated November 8, 2026.

Freeport is an incorporated village in the Town of Hempstead and, with 44,472 people in 2020, the largest of these six towns. Its LIRR station is on the Babylon Branch. Freeport Public Schools serves the village, Freeport runs its own electric utility, and the Nautical Mile on Woodcleft Canal is its waterfront.

What should sellers in Freeport know?

Buyers looking at Freeport ask about the same few things this page covers: the flood zone, the school district, taxes and the commute. Have your answers ready before you list.

  • Price from closed sales in your part of Freeport, not asking prices. OneKey MLS reports by county, and Nassau County's monthly median single-family price ran from $835,000 to $911,000 in 2026 through September.
  • Complete the New York property condition disclosure, flood questions included, before your first showing. Since March 20, 2024 it cannot be replaced with a $500 credit.
  • Have your flood papers and village electric bills ready. Freeport is a canal village with its own electric utility, and buyers ask about both.
  • Have your tax bills ready. Prepaid or unpaid taxes are adjusted with the buyer at closing, and buyers budget by monthly cost.

What should buyers in Freeport know?

  • Look up the exact address on FEMA's Flood Map Service Center. FEMA says nearly 25 percent of flood insurance claims come from outside high-risk flood zones, so a low-risk map is not the end of the question.
  • Confirm the school district for the street with the district, not the listing.
  • Freeport is the largest of these six towns, with the most housing units, and it has the lowest owner-estimated median home value of the six in Census figures.
  • Since August 17, 2024, agents who are REALTORS need a written buyer agreement before touring a home with you.
  • Register for New York's STAR credit with the state tax department once the home is your primary residence.

Is Freeport a village or part of the Town of Hempstead?

Freeport is an incorporated village with its own government and village taxes. It has run its own electric utility since 1898, which gives residents lower rates than many surrounding communities. Ask the seller for recent electric bills.

Which school district serves a home in Freeport?

Freeport Public Schools operates the village's public schools: eight schools and 6,343 students in the 2023 to 2024 school year. Confirm the district for the exact address.

How do property taxes work in Freeport?

Freeport is an incorporated village, so a home there pays village tax on top of Town of Hempstead, Nassau County and school taxes. The village bills its own tax separately.

The Town of Hempstead Receiver of Taxes collects school and general taxes in halves. General taxes are due January 1 and July 1, payable without penalty through February 10 and August 10. School taxes are due October 1 and April 1, payable without penalty through November 10 and May 10.

Nassau County assesses homes at 0.1 percent of its market value estimate. Owners who think the estimate is too high can grieve with the county's Assessment Review Commission. The next window runs January 4 to March 1, 2027, online through AROW. Sellers can read how property taxes and grievances affect a sale.

What kinds of homes are in Freeport?

The Census Bureau counts 14,793 housing units in Freeport. Owners there estimated the median value of their homes at $505,900, in the American Community Survey 2020 to 2024 five-year estimates. That figure is what owners said, averaged over five years, so it lags the market and is not a sale price. For sale prices, use closed sales.

What do people ask about Freeport?

Is Freeport a village?

Yes. Freeport residents voted to incorporate the village on October 18, 1892. It sits in the Town of Hempstead.

Does Freeport have its own electric company?

Yes. Freeport has run a municipal electric utility since 1898.

Which LIRR branch serves Freeport?

The Babylon Branch.

What is the Nautical Mile?

Freeport's waterfront strip along the west side of Woodcleft Canal, with seafood restaurants, shops and boat rides. A festival is held there the first weekend in June.
