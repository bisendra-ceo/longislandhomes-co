Oceanside, NY real estate guide

By Bisendra Melaram, SRS, PSA, SRES, ABR, SFR, MRP, AHWD, RSPS, Licensed Real Estate Salesperson, Keller Williams Greater Nassau. Last updated November 8, 2026.

Oceanside is an unincorporated hamlet in the Town of Hempstead, with 32,637 residents in 2020 and its own school district. Its LIRR station is on the Long Beach Branch. Hurricane Sandy flooded the southern part of Oceanside in 2012, so the flood zone and the cost of flood insurance belong at the top of any buyer's checklist.

What should sellers in Oceanside know?

Buyers looking at Oceanside ask about the same few things this page covers: the flood zone, the school district, taxes and the commute. Have your answers ready before you list.

  • Price from closed sales in your part of Oceanside, not asking prices. OneKey MLS reports by county, and Nassau County's monthly median single-family price ran from $835,000 to $911,000 in 2026 through September.
  • Complete the New York property condition disclosure, flood questions included, before your first showing. Since March 20, 2024 it cannot be replaced with a $500 credit.
  • Expect flood questions first. Sandy flooded the southern part of the hamlet in 2012, so have your flood insurance policy, any elevation certificate and your claims history ready.
  • Have your tax bills ready. Prepaid or unpaid taxes are adjusted with the buyer at closing, and buyers budget by monthly cost.

What should buyers in Oceanside know?

  • Look up the exact address on FEMA's Flood Map Service Center. FEMA says nearly 25 percent of flood insurance claims come from outside high-risk flood zones, so a low-risk map is not the end of the question.
  • Confirm the school district for the street with the district, not the listing.
  • Get a flood insurance quote before you sign. Oceanside has the longest direct morning ride of these six towns, a median 43.5 minutes to Penn Station.
  • Since August 17, 2024, agents who are REALTORS need a written buyer agreement before touring a home with you.
  • Register for New York's STAR credit with the state tax department once the home is your primary residence.

How do you commute from Oceanside to Manhattan?

Oceanside station sits at Weidner Avenue and Lawson Boulevard on the Long Beach Branch. Under the MTA's 2023 service plan, Long Beach Branch peak trains run to both Penn Station and Grand Central Madison, and all of them stop at Jamaica.

Is Oceanside in a flood zone?

Flood risk is the first question in Oceanside. Hurricane Sandy completely flooded the southern part of the hamlet in 2012, and power was out for nearly two weeks after a nearby substation in Island Park failed. Only Reynolds Channel and marsh islands separate Oceanside from the barrier island and the ocean. Look up the exact address on FEMA's map, get a flood insurance quote before you sign a contract, and ask whether the home has an elevation certificate.

Since March 20, 2024, New York sellers must answer flood questions on the property condition disclosure, including whether the home is in a FEMA flood zone, whether flood insurance is required, and whether there have been flood claims. Is Oceanside a village or part of the Town of Hempstead?

Oceanside is a hamlet, not a village. It has no village government or village tax. Zoning, permits and building questions go to the Town of Hempstead.

Which school district serves a home in Oceanside?

Oceanside is served by the Oceanside School District. Part of neighboring Rockville Centre is also in the Oceanside district.

How do property taxes work in Oceanside?

Oceanside is a hamlet, so there is no village tax. Zoning, permits and town taxes run through the Town of Hempstead.

The Town of Hempstead Receiver of Taxes collects school and general taxes in halves. General taxes are due January 1 and July 1, payable without penalty through February 10 and August 10. School taxes are due October 1 and April 1, payable without penalty through November 10 and May 10.

Nassau County assesses homes at 0.1 percent of its market value estimate. Owners who think the estimate is too high can grieve with the county's Assessment Review Commission. The next window runs January 4 to March 1, 2027, online through AROW. Sellers can read how property taxes and grievances affect a sale.

What kinds of homes are in Oceanside?

The Census Bureau counts 11,328 housing units in Oceanside. Owners there estimated the median value of their homes at $634,300, in the American Community Survey 2020 to 2024 five-year estimates. That figure is what owners said, averaged over five years, so it lags the market and is not a sale price. For sale prices, use closed sales.
