About this guide

By Bisendra Melaram, SRS, PSA, SRES, ABR, SFR, MRP, AHWD, RSPS, Licensed Real Estate Salesperson, Keller Williams Greater Nassau. Last updated November 8, 2026.

Long Island Homes is a free town guide to the South Shore of Long Island for sellers and buyers, written by Bisendra Melaram, a seller's agent and New York Licensed Real Estate Salesperson since 2003 with Keller Williams Greater Nassau in Garden City. Each town page cites its sources and carries the date it was last updated.

How are the facts checked?

Town facts come from public sources listed at the bottom of each page: Census figures, LIRR and MTA information, FEMA flood maps, village and school district sites, and New York law. Market numbers come from the county reports OneKey MLS publishes each month, with the report month and date shown next to every figure. Pages are refreshed monthly.

How does this guide handle fair housing?

It describes places, never the people who live in them. Fair housing law bars agents from steering anyone toward or away from a neighborhood.
