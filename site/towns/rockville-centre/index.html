Rockville Centre, NY real estate guide

By Bisendra Melaram, SRS, PSA, SRES, ABR, SFR, MRP, AHWD, RSPS, Licensed Real Estate Salesperson, Keller Williams Greater Nassau. Last updated November 8, 2026.

Rockville Centre is an incorporated village in the Town of Hempstead, with 26,016 residents in 2020. Its LIRR station is on the Babylon Branch. Most homes are in the Rockville Centre school district, but about a fifth of residents are in the Oceanside district and some are in Baldwin's. The village runs its own electric utility.

What should sellers in Rockville Centre know?

Buyers looking at Rockville Centre ask about the same few things this page covers: the flood zone, the school district, taxes and the commute. Have your answers ready before you list.

  • Price from closed sales in your part of Rockville Centre, not asking prices. OneKey MLS reports by county, and Nassau County's monthly median single-family price ran from $835,000 to $911,000 in 2026 through September.
  • Complete the New York property condition disclosure, flood questions included, before your first showing. Since March 20, 2024 it cannot be replaced with a $500 credit.
  • Have your village electric bills ready. The village runs its own electric department, and buyers will ask what power costs.

What should buyers in Rockville Centre know?

  • Look up the exact address on FEMA's Flood Map Service Center. FEMA says nearly 25 percent of flood insurance claims come from outside high-risk flood zones, so a low-risk map is not the end of the question.

Is Rockville Centre in a flood zone?

Most of Rockville Centre sits north of the bayfront towns, but parts of the village do contain water area, and FEMA maps flood zones lot by lot. Check the exact address before you bid.

Since March 20, 2024, New York sellers must answer flood questions on the property condition disclosure, including whether the home is in a FEMA flood zone, whether flood insurance is required, and whether there have been flood claims. Is Rockville Centre a village or part of the Town of Hempstead?

Rockville Centre is an incorporated village with its own government and village taxes. It also runs its own electric department, which the village says lets it offer low-cost power to customers. Ask the seller for recent electric bills.

Which school district serves a home in Rockville Centre?

Most of the village is in the Rockville Centre Union Free School District, which also reaches into parts of South Hempstead. About 20 percent of residents live in the Oceanside district and part of the village is in the Baldwin district. Two houses on nearby streets can pay different school taxes, so confirm the district for the exact address.

Districts that serve parts of Rockville Centre, with official sites where checked:

How do property taxes work in Rockville Centre?

Rockville Centre is an incorporated village, so a home there pays village tax on top of Town of Hempstead, Nassau County and school taxes. The village bills its own tax separately.

The Town of Hempstead Receiver of Taxes collects school and general taxes in halves. General taxes are due January 1 and July 1, payable without penalty through February 10 and August 10. School taxes are due October 1 and April 1, payable without penalty through November 10 and May 10.

Nassau County assesses homes at 0.1 percent of its market value estimate. Owners who think the estimate is too high can grieve with the county's Assessment Review Commission. The next window runs January 4 to March 1, 2027, online through AROW. Sellers can read how property taxes and grievances affect a sale.

What kinds of homes are in Rockville Centre?

The Census Bureau counts 10,332 housing units in Rockville Centre. Owners there estimated the median value of their homes at $818,700, in the American Community Survey 2020 to 2024 five-year estimates. That figure is what owners said, averaged over five years, so it lags the market and is not a sale price. For sale prices, use closed sales.
