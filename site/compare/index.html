How do the South Shore towns compare?

By Bisendra Melaram, SRS, PSA, SRES, ABR, SFR, MRP, AHWD, RSPS, Licensed Real Estate Salesperson, Keller Williams Greater Nassau. Last updated November 8, 2026.

Valley Stream has the shortest direct morning train ride of these six towns, a median 34 minutes to Penn Station, and Oceanside the longest at 43.5. Oceanside and Baldwin are hamlets with no village tax. Rockville Centre and Freeport run their own electric utilities. Freeport has the most housing units of the six, by Census count.

What does village or hamlet mean for your taxes?

A home in an incorporated village pays village tax on top of Town of Hempstead, Nassau County and school taxes, and the village runs its own building department. A hamlet has no village government, so zoning and permits go through the Town of Hempstead. All six towns pay school and general taxes to the Town of Hempstead Receiver of Taxes. See the glossary for terms.

Why can two homes in the same town pay different school taxes?

Because South Shore school district lines do not follow village or hamlet lines. Parts of Lynbrook are in five districts, and about a fifth of Rockville Centre is in the Oceanside district. Each town guide lists the districts that serve it.

What else do people ask?

Which South Shore town has the shortest train ride to Manhattan?

Of these six, Valley Stream. Direct weekday morning trains from Valley Stream station to Penn Station had a median scheduled ride of 34 minutes in the November 2026 LIRR schedule, and Oceanside's had the longest, 43.5 minutes.

Which South Shore towns have no village tax?

Oceanside and Baldwin. Both are hamlets governed by the Town of Hempstead. Valley Stream, Lynbrook, Rockville Centre and Freeport are incorporated villages with their own village taxes.

Which South Shore villages run their own electric utility?

Rockville Centre and Freeport. Each village runs a municipal electric department, so buyers there should ask the seller for recent electric bills.

Are the home values in this comparison sale prices?

No. They are owners' own estimates from the Census Bureau's American Community Survey, averaged over 2020 to 2024. They lag the market. For sale prices, use recent closed sales.
