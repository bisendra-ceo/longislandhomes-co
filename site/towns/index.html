South Shore town guides

By Bisendra Melaram, SRS, PSA, SRES, ABR, SFR, MRP, AHWD, RSPS, Licensed Real Estate Salesperson, Keller Williams Greater Nassau. Last updated November 8, 2026.

These guides compare six South Shore towns in Nassau County for sellers and buyers: Valley Stream, Lynbrook, Rockville Centre, Oceanside, Baldwin and Freeport. Each covers the LIRR stations and branch, the flood zone question, which school districts serve which streets, and whether the town is a village with its own taxes.

How do the six towns compare?

TownGovernmentLIRR
Valley StreamIncorporated village since February 14Valley Stream station (Far Rockaway and Long Beach branches, fare zone 4); Gibson station (Far Rockaway Branch)
LynbrookIncorporated village since 1911Lynbrook station (Long Beach Branch and select Babylon Branch trains); Westwood station (West Hempstead Branch) at the Lynbrook and Malverne line
Rockville CentreIncorporated village since July 15Rockville Centre station (Babylon Branch)
OceansideHamlet (no village government)Oceanside station (Long Beach Branch), at Weidner Avenue and Lawson Boulevard
BaldwinHamlet (no village government)Baldwin station (Babylon Branch)
FreeportIncorporated village since 1892Freeport station (Babylon Branch)
